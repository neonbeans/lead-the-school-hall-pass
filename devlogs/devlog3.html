Devlog 26.8.1

August 26th, 2026

Huge upgrades and updates!

I am finally back with a new and exciting update for Hall Pass! This update will finally help establish this rendition as its own unique entity with some Lead the School elements.

Some of those things that I've added are...

  • New character + fight

    • There is now a second boss fight! Unlock it by beating Edgar *and see what she has in store!*

  • New currency system

    • Say hello to gold stars! These will be the main currency to Hall Pass and will help you obtain certain accessories in the far future.

  • New results screen

    • After winning a fight, you'll be granted a results screen with a ranking of how well you fought *just like in the original games!*

  • Statistics menu for DMG and rounds

    • I added a menu of all of your stats for those nerds who want every action they've ever done in Hall Pass.

  • Revamped startup screen

    • A new menu screen has been made to reflect all of these changes and has been stylized to fit more into the Lead the School aesthetic.

  • Flavor text for when you click on Eric in the Freeplay menu

    • When you click Eric in the Freeplay menu, you'll see descriptions for each fight you have selected.

  • Saving, loading and resetting data

    • This one is probably the most important of all; you can now save your data! This will ensure that everyone is satisfied and keeps playing to get better without having to start all over again.

    In conclusion...

    I can't believe I've done so much to this game already. It feels like yesterday that I thought of a Lead the School retelling, and yet it's come so far. I'm very proud of this update and how everything turned out in the end. More updates like this will come.

    Thank you for taking the time to read my devlog, and make sure to check out my other work!

    -Neon
