July 14th, 2026

Lead the School Update!

I've decided to continue with this little remaster of Lead the School and turn it into something I actually want to do. This update may be small, but it'll help expand this game into something more than what it was before.

Some of those things that I've added are...

  • Changed the name from "Lead the School Revisioned" to "Lead the School - Hall Pass"

    • This will help differentiate it from being just another remaster I'm doing and turn it into something genuine.

  • Changed update format

    • With this update format, it goes like this: Year, Month and Update. With this format, it’ll be easier to remember dates and how many updates I’ve done throughout each month.

  • Cooldown timer

    • This will help with indiciating when your card will be next played. I plan on making this optional in the settings if people prefer having no cooldown.

  • Gum card added

    • I've added the original Gum card from the original game as well as altered it's mechanic to better suit my version.

  • Added descriptions for cards

    • Along with the gum card, since switching out cards is actually useful I've added some descrptions for cards (that includes their cooldown and how much DMG they do).

  • Revamped settings menu

    • I've turned the "mute" button into volume sliders for music and sound, as well as added a "Show DMG Numbers" button.

    I can't say when I'm adding the next boss just yet; it could be next update, or it could be next month. But I'm actively brainstorming and adding new mechanics and ideas to this game to slowly make it more replayable.

    Thank you for taking the time to read my devlog, and make sure to check out my other work!

    -Neon
