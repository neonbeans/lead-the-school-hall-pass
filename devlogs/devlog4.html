Devlog 26.9.1

September 16th, 2026

Very minor update

New month, which means new Hall Pass update. This update is very small due to real-life complications and other factors, but I made sure not to fully disappoint.

Some of the things that I added are...

  • New leaderboard

    • I added a leaderboard for Newgrounds players of Hall Pass showing how many gold stars you have. The podium will have the top 5 richest players.

  • Randomized DMG numbers

    • I changed how getting damage works, with each attack being slightly randomized. This will tie into the next big Hall Pass update coming in October.

  • Revamped buttons and fonts

    • I added new fonts and made everything more visually pleasing, while getting rid of the pixelated font indefinitely.

    In conclusion...

    One important thing to mention that connects to the leaderboard system; the definitive version of Hall Pass will be on Newgrounds. I plan to move away from itch.io and create a separate website for Hall Pass content, which will make the page obsolete. Despite this, I want to eventually work towards a real application for Hall Pass. But that won't be for a while.

    Thank you for taking the time to read my devlog, and make sure to check out my other work!

    -Neon
